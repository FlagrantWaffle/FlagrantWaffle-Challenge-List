## Recording Requirements

1. All clicks must be audible.
2. The full completion must be shown.
3. The recording must be continuous (no cuts or edits)
4. The recording must be a legible quality

## Proof Requirements

1. Proof must be accessible via the link provided
2. The submitted video must clearly show the completed level.
3. The "level complete" screen must be clearly visible along with the cheat indicator where applicable

## General Rules

1. Cheating or illegitimate completions are not allowed.
2. Moderators may request additional evidence where necessary.
3. Moderator decisions may be appealed according to the site rules.
