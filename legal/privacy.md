## 1. Information We Collect

### Discord Account Information

The Service uses Discord OAuth for authentication.

When you sign in, the Service may receive and store information provided by Discord, including:

- your Discord user ID;
- Discord username;
- Discord display name;
- Discord avatar or avatar identifier; and
- other basic account information made available through the authorised Discord OAuth scopes.

Your Discord user ID is used as the stable identifier linking your Discord account to your Service account.

It may also be used by the FlagrantWaffle Discord bot to mention the correct Discord account when communicating submission-review results.

The Service does not require or receive your Discord password.

### Service Account Information

The Service may store:

- an internal account ID;
- administrator status;
- moderation statistics such as the number of submissions reviewed by an administrator;
- Waffle Point totals derived from approved completions; and
- profile information used by the Service.

### Submission and Completion Information

When you submit a challenge completion, the Service may store:

- the level submitted;
- the proof URL;
- the submission date and time;
- submission status;
- the resulting completion record if approved;
- completion date;
- moderator comments;
- review date and time;
- the moderator who reviewed the submission; and
- related moderation history.

Every reviewed submission includes a moderator comment explaining the reason for the review outcome.

### Technical Information

The Service may process limited technical information required to operate and secure the website.

This may include:

- IP addresses;
- request timestamps;
- browser or device information;
- HTTP request information;
- security logs; and
- error or diagnostic information.

This information may be processed directly by the Service or by infrastructure providers such as Cloudflare, hosting services or reverse-proxy software.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

Personal information is used to:

- authenticate users through Discord;
- maintain Service accounts;
- display Discord profile information such as profile pictures;
- associate submissions and completions with the correct user;
- operate challenge leaderboards and Waffle Points;
- review submitted completions;
- maintain moderation and submission history;
- allow previous records to be re-checked where necessary;
- prevent duplicate, fraudulent or abusive submissions;
- operate administrator and moderation tools;
- provide Discord notifications when submissions are reviewed;
- mention the correct user in Discord using their Discord user ID;
- maintain security and prevent abuse;
- investigate technical problems; and
- operate and improve the Service.

Personal information is not sold to advertisers.

## 3. Discord Review Notifications

When a submission is approved or denied, information about the review may be posted by the FlagrantWaffle bot in a dedicated Discord channel.

The notification may include:

- your Discord mention;
- your Discord identity;
- the submitted level;
- the review verdict;
- the mandatory moderator comment explaining the outcome;
- the reviewing moderator;
- the proof URL; and
- other information necessary to communicate the review outcome.

Members who can access that Discord channel may therefore see this information.

## 4. Public Information

Certain information may be publicly visible through the Service, including:

- usernames;
- profile pictures;
- approved completions;
- Waffle Points;
- leaderboard positions;
- completion dates;
- proof links; and
- other information associated with public challenge achievements.

Pending submissions and private moderation information are intended to be restricted to the relevant user and authorised moderators except where information is published through the review-notification process described above.

## 5. Proof Availability

Proof submitted for an approved completion is intended to remain accessible so that recognised records can be reviewed and verified in the future where necessary.

The Service may periodically access or display submitted proof URLs.

If a proof video or other submitted evidence is deleted, made private, becomes inaccessible or is otherwise no longer available through the submitted link, the corresponding completion may be removed, voided or marked as no longer verified.

This may also affect associated leaderboard positions or Waffle Point totals.
